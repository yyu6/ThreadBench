The study retains 436 annotators and evaluates 240 matched pairs: eight domains × three systems × ten pairs. Each pair presents a real discussion and a generated discussion for the same post.

04 / EVALUATION SETUP

Separate pools.
Shared protocol.

The real corpus is divided into non-overlapping seed, reference and test pools for each domain. All three methods use real seed discussions for generation. ThreadPDG additionally uses reference-pool information for profiles, targets and composition; its main draft budget is K = 3.

ThreadBench compares the generated and held-out real metric distributions with two-sided MWU and KS tests. The paper also reports Wasserstein-1 distance and absolute Cliff’s delta. In the repeated real-vs-real sanity check, both tests pass in 94.30% of metric comparisons.
